5 FATALITIES RUNWAY OVERRUN DCA26MA352 SEPT 6, 2026 BOEING 767-33AER(BDSF)

21 Air Flight 7598 Overrun — Miami (MIA)

Scheduled domestic cargo flight from San Juan (SJU) to Miami (MIA) operating for Amazon Air.

Accident Summary: On September 6, 2026, at 13:53 EDT, a 32-year-old Boeing 767 freighter overran Runway 30 at Miami International Airport following an unstable high-speed approach. The aircraft traveled ~1,300 feet past the paved end, crashed through the perimeter fence, crossed NW 67th Avenue, and struck two moving vehicles — obliterating an airport cleaning crew van and crashing into an SUV. 5 people on the ground were killed (all occupants of the van) and 5 others were injured, including both pilots.

Critical Findings & Aftermath

  • No Deployed Drag Systems: Telemetry confirms SPEED BRAKES (spoilers) and THRUST REVERSERS were never deployed during the landing roll.
  • Fatal Go-Around Hesitation: Releasing brakes at 120 knots to attempt a go-around late on the runway, then immediately reversing decision 4 seconds later, stripped the aircraft of vital stopping distance.
  • Lack of EMAS Protection: Runway 30 lacked an Engineered Materials Arrestor System (EMAS). Even if installed, standard EMAS beds are rated for overruns under 70 knots, whereas N1997A departed pavement at ~96 knots.
  • Legal & Commercial Action: On Sept 9, 2026, a wrongful death lawsuit was filed against Amazon.com Inc. and 21 Air LLC alleging gross negligence and poor training. On Sept 14, 2026, Amazon officially suspended all operations with 21 Air.
